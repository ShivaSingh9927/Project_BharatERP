# Pricing Strategy — India ERP

> **Last Updated:** July 3, 2026
> **Purpose:** Pricing tiers, revenue model, competitor comparison

---

## Pricing Philosophy

1. **Self-serve first** — Users can try free, upgrade without talking to sales
2. **India pricing** — Charge in ₹ INR, not USD converted
3. **Value-based** — Price based on value delivered (time saved, compliance automated)
4. **Land and expand** — Start free, grow with customer's business
5. **Undercut everyone** — Campfire charges $5K-15K/mo. We charge ₹0-30K/mo

## Why This Pricing Works

1. **Free tier** = user acquisition (Campfire has none — they lose this whole segment)
2. **Starter ₹3K/mo** = cheaper than Tally Prime annual + someone's salary to operate Tally
3. **Growth ₹10K/mo** = still **80x cheaper** than Campfire ($120 vs $10,000/mo)
4. **Enterprise ₹30K+** = for large groups who need custom work (still 25x cheaper than Campfire)
5. **Self-serve** = zero sales overhead, product sells itself
