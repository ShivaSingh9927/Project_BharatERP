> **Created:** July 3, 2026

> **Inspiration:** Campfire.ai (campfire.ai) — $103.5M raised, AI-native ERP for US SaaS companies

## What We're Building

An AI-powered accounting and finance platform (ERP) built specifically for Indian businesses — with all the AI automation of Campfire, but with India's compliance requirements (GST, TDS, e-invoicing, e-way bills) at the core, not bolted on.

## One-Sentence Pitch

> *"The first AI-native ERP built for India — GST/TDS/e-invoicing compliant out of the box, with AI that speaks your language, at a price Indian businesses can actually afford."*
